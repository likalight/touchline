            What you're watching

            A fully synthetic match between two fictional clubs, generated by our own football simulator. No real league data is used. Every pass, carry, shot and tackle arrives as a live event with a tracking snapshot of all 22 players.

            Agents turn that feed into explained moments. They don't just say a goal happened: they say how likely it was, what made it likely, what would have changed it, and how much it moved the result. Every claim links to the events it came from.

            The same moment is written four ways: for a casual fan, an analyst, a studio lower-third, and an audio description for blind and low-vision fans. You can pick a club, follow a player or track a single number.
